## Paper summary

The paper writes graph ANN-to-SNN conversion error as an exact per-layer identity whose
aggregation operator sits inside the error, proves a four-part theorem (exact identity, membrane
identity, quantisation reachability, `rho_n`-weighted L-layer bound with a mandatory bias term),
and separates that deterministic, intra-aggregation mechanism from the cross-layer stochastic
misalignment of ICML'25 with a controlled 2x2 probe and a four-way neuron/bias control. It then
attempts to invert the bound into a per-model timestep rule, **falsifies that inversion**, and
reports a conservatism-calibrated feasibility band with an explicit failure boundary. Measured on
seven seeds: the two-phase TPP transplant trails the closed-form correction by 4.24 pp at T=4;
inter-layer permutation changes accuracy by +0.54 pp; intra-aggregation phase perturbation costs
11.88 pp; the bound-derived screening rule is correct on 3/3 held-out graphs and saves 50.0% of
timesteps on the evaluable ones; a shuffled-`kappa` control drops screening accuracy from 1.00 to
0.50. The strict pre-registered strong criterion is **not met**, and the manuscript says so.
