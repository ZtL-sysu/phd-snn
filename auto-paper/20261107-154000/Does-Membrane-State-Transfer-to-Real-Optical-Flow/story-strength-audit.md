# Story strength audit (round 001)

Why a skeptical reviewer cares: a +0.28 spiking-state gain measured only on synthetic moving dots
may be an artifact of regular motion. A same-protocol synthetic-versus-real comparison settles
whether the mechanism transfers, and the answer is informative under either sign.

Strongest paper making this look incremental: arXiv:2604.04033 (topology/null-model verdict) and
arXiv:2610.10023 (structure alone suffices). Distinguishing experiment: the domain-gap comparison
plus one-factor mechanism ablation under matched controls, which neither paper runs.

Threshold for a strong paper: real-domain `delta >= +0.25` with CI lower bound > 0, a decisive
transferability verdict, and at least one ablation identifying the carrier mechanism; then P5/P5A
acceptance, P9 full review `**Overall:** X >= 8.0` and P21 delivery.

If only partially positive: an honest diagnostic paper stating that the membrane-state gain is
specific to synthetic regular motion, with the null-model boundary and scale-stability constraints,
aimed at the rescue venue family. This is a genuine contribution, not a failed run to hide.

Agreement with the feasibility contract: yes. No experiment is promised beyond the data, compute,
seed and ablation budgets recorded there. The independent review's guardrail-4 framing risk is
carried here as a known weakness and addressed by the venue-valued transferability verdict.
