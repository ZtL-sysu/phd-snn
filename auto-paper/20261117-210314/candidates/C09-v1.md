# C09-v1 — uncertainty of complementary low-step prefixes

Date frozen: 2026-11-17 before outcome evaluation.

## Hypothesis

A causal score combining T1/T2 prediction disagreement and T2 top-two margin identifies graphs for which four-step conversion is worth the extra work. The decision is made only from early converted prefixes, never labels or future full-conversion logits. Thresholds and upgrade fractions are fit on a disjoint tuning partition.

## Smoke task

Use the fixed-GCN full-split validation prediction archives for SuperPixel MNIST and CIFAR10. They provide paired teacher, T1, T2 and T4 logits for all 5,000 official validation graphs. Split IDs deterministically into 512 development, 512 tuning and 512 internal validation graphs; test archives are not read.

## Controls

- Uniform T2 and uniform T4.
- Random upgrade at the method's selected fraction, 2,000 fixed-seed draws.
- T2-margin-only selection at the same fraction.
- Same prefix and same paired IDs, no tuning on internal validation.

## Metrics and gate

Primary: graph accuracy and teacher agreement. Secondary: mean decoded T under the declared model `T = 2 + 2 * upgraded_fraction`, per-example wins/losses, paired bootstrap interval.

Minimum gate: on each dataset, C09 improves accuracy or agreement over T2 by at least +0.2 percentage points and is not below random's 95th-percentile accuracy at the same fraction.

Strong gate: on both datasets, C09 beats both random and margin-only accuracy at the same fraction, achieves within 0.5 pp of uniform T4 teacher agreement while mean T≤3.0, and has a nonnegative paired accuracy difference versus T2 on the majority of bootstrap draws (95% lower bound > -0.5 pp).

Failure: minimum gate misses on either dataset, cost exceeds 3.0 mean T, or selection improves accuracy only at the expense of teacher agreement by more than 1 pp. Stop this version; a revised score is C09-v2 with a new frozen gate.

## Novelty status

`needs-search`: dynamic/SNN early exit and temporal coding are established adjacent areas; novelty must be tied to causal uncertainty of converted prefixes and graph-message work rather than generic adaptive inference.
