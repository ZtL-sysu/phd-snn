# C10-v1 — operator-conditioned threshold calibration for graph conversion

Date frozen: 2026-11-17 before outcome evaluation.

## Hypothesis

Different graph aggregation hosts (`H1_mean`, `H2_sum`, `H3_sym`) induce different normalized message gains and therefore different rate-conversion bias. A scalar per-host threshold/gain factor fit on the training split from teacher-logit fidelity will transfer to the fixed validation split and improve low-T conversion versus the universal threshold.

## Data and model

Use the saved 2-layer hidden-16 checkpoint family in `Intra-Aggregation-Misalignment-Graph-Conversion/f-research-v1-run-20261105-111502-gnn2snn/results/models`, with Chameleon and Squirrel. Load public fixed splits through `graph_data.load_ds`; evaluate only validation labels/logits. The checkpoint's teacher logits are recomputed from its frozen weights. Fit factor on training nodes only, no test masks.

## Controls

- Universal threshold `theta=1`.
- Same checkpoint and graph operator with random scalar factor drawn from the training grid.
- The strongest of the three host-specific factors with factor selected by training agreement, then transferred to both other hosts (to test whether operator identity matters).
- T=2 is primary because T=4/8 can hide low-T conversion differences.

## Metrics and gate

Primary: validation accuracy and teacher agreement at T=2. Secondary: teacher logit MSE, spike count/node, factor stability across seeds.

Minimum gate: on each dataset, operator-conditioned factor beats universal threshold by at least +1.0 percentage point accuracy and improves teacher agreement on the same split.

Strong gate: improvement over universal threshold on both datasets and at least two of three hosts; beat shuffled/random-factor control at matched factor count; paired bootstrap lower bound > 0 for accuracy difference; no >10% spike-count increase.

Failure: either dataset misses minimum, factor selected on training does not transfer to validation, or any gain requires more than 10% extra spikes. Stop C10-v1; a different graph-structure mechanism is C10-v2.

## Novelty status

`needs-search`: generic threshold calibration and A²Q exist; the narrow claim is operator-conditioned graph-message calibration and a frozen host-transfer test. No novelty is claimed from scalar calibration alone.
