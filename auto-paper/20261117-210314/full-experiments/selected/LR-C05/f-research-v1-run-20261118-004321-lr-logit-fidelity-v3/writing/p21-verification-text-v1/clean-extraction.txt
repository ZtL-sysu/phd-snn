                                                Abstract
    Frozen GNN-to-SNN conversion can require repeated transport of the first-layer input message
M0 = ÂX. Under a fixed serialization budget, low-rank coding may omit residual directions that matter
to the frozen model’s logits. We therefore ask whether magnitude-weighted output-Jacobian allocation
of sparse exact residual coefficients improves logit fidelity at matched total padded serialized bits while
limiting observed task degradation. On the reported test split, rank-128 transport with a 2% residual
budget reduced the ratio of three-seed mean split-specific logit MSE relative to the strongest evaluated
matched-budget residual control by 26.23%, 33.99%, and 25.83% on Cora, Chameleon, and Squirrel; all
nine dataset-seed comparisons improved. Against our matched-budget A2Q-style and One-Spike-style
adaptations, the reductions were 28.93%, 36.19%, and 35.24%, and 42.83%, 47.61%, and 34.03%, re-
spectively. Across T = 2/4 accuracy and teacher agreement, the maximum observed three-seed-mean
degradation was 1.86 percentage points and the maximum seed-level degradation was 2.85 percentage
points relative to the best matched-budget non-proposed control for each dataset and task metric. These
are descriptive maxima, not equivalence results or population bounds. Compared methods used identical
total padded serialized bits within each dataset at rank 128 and 2%. A separate rank128/1% warmed
benchmark measured paired p95 full-pipeline runtime ratios of 1.020-1.124 versus SVD-only and peak
CUDA allocation of 218.23 MB; it measures wall-clock cost, not energy or focal 2% timing. The evi-
dence is held-out confirmation after validation-exposed setting selection, not preregistered or independent
replication, and the contribution is limited to logit-fidelity coding of M0 = ÂX.

1     Introduction
Frozen GNN-to-SNN compression repeatedly transports high-dimensional first-layer graph messages. Low-
rank transport can preserve large message components while omitting residual directions that influence the
frozen model’s outputs. This creates a specific output-sensitive coding problem: under a fixed serialization
budget, which sparse residual coefficients should be retained to improve logit fidelity without exceeding the
transport cost?
    We study this question for the transported input message M0 = ÂX only. The evaluated method retains
an operator-aligned rank-128 basis and allocates a 2% sparse residual budget using the composite score
si = ri2 ∥Ji ∥22 , where ri is the residual coefficient and Ji is the analytic derivative of the frozen ANN logits
with respect to the corresponding transported message entry under the ReLU hidden mask. Selection is
global for each dataset and checkpoint seed over the full transductive graph and does not use labels. The
sparse coefficients are exact message residual values with explicit indices; they are not temporal spikes. The
second aggregation ÂH (1) and output layer are not compressed by this method.
    The reported test run confirms lower logit MSE on all three graphs at matched total padded serialized bits.
At rank 128 and 2%, the ratio of three-seed means fell by 26.23%, 33.99%, and 25.83% on Cora, Chameleon,
and Squirrel relative to the strongest evaluated matched-budget residual control. Task superiority was
not established: task deltas were mixed across the evaluated budget grid, and the earlier task-superiority
target failed. The supported task statement is instead a descriptive maximum observed degradation of
1.86 percentage points across three-seed means and 2.85 percentage points at seed level under per-metric
matched-budget comparisons.


2     Related Work
Graph neural networks provide the frozen message-passing substrate for this study. Graph convolution,
neighborhood sampling, attention, expressive message passing, depth, and higher-order mixing are estab-
lished by a broad line of work Kipf and Welling [2017], Hamilton et al. [2017], Veličković et al. [2018], Xu
et al. [2019], Li et al. [2019], Abu-El-Haija et al. [2019], Chen et al. [2020], Li et al. [2018], Wu et al. [2021].
These papers motivate the normalized input message M0 = ÂX, but they do not study exact serialized-bit
allocation for frozen conversion. The present paper therefore compresses one concrete operator output rather
than proposing a new graph architecture.
    Graph-model compression changes what is communicated or stored. Quantization-aware GNN training,
vector-quantized node representations, tensor-core-oriented quantized execution, aggregation-aware mixed
precision, distributed message quantization, quantized conversion, general quantization, and low-rank neural
compression cover complementary cost models Tailor et al. [2021], Ding et al. [2021], Wang et al. [2022b],
Zhu et al. [2023], Wan et al. [2023], Hu et al. [2023], Gholami et al. [2022], Cherukuri and Lala [2025]. These
methods differ in learned codebooks, bitwidth allocation, communication primitives, training objectives, and
hardware assumptions. Our matched-budget A2Q-style and One-Spike-style adaptations are evaluated only
under the present padded serialized-bit ledger; they are not direct reproductions of the published systems.
    ANN-to-SNN conversion studies accuracy-latency trade-offs, offset and group-neuron corrections, early
exit, transformer conversion, and post-training calibration Hwang and Kung [2025], Hao et al. [2023], Lv

frozen graph messages, can magnitude-weighted output-Jacobian residual allocation improve logit fidelity at
matched total padded serialized-bit cost? The present contribution is limited to that question on M0 = ÂX
for frozen two-layer hidden-16 GCN checkpoints on Cora, Chameleon, and Squirrel.

4     Experiments and Results
4.1    Experimental Scope
We evaluate Cora, Chameleon, and Squirrel using the reported split protocol and frozen two-layer hidden-
16 GCN checkpoints with seeds 0, 1, and 2. Validation exposed ranks 16, 32, 64, and 128 and residual
budgets 0.5%, 1%, and 2%. Guidance-v3 fixed rank 128 and the 2% focal budget after validation exposure
and before the confirmation test run. The reported test is therefore held-out confirmation after validation-
exposed reframing and setting selection, not preregistered or independent replication. No focal-setting
selection based on the test results is claimed.

4.4    Sensitivity and Matched-Budget Controls
At rank 128 and the 2% residual budget, hidden-state MSE fell by 16.88%, 12.86%, and 10.63% relative to the
strongest matched-budget control on Cora, Chameleon, and Squirrel. These three-seed mean reductions are
descriptive sensitivity measurements without formal confidence intervals and were obtained in the validation-
grid analysis preceding held-out confirmation of the focal setting.
    Across the evaluated 0.5%, 1%, and 2% residual budgets and three graphs, logit MSE was below the
strongest corresponding matched-budget residual control in all nine dataset-budget cells, with reductions
from 2.98% to 30.40%. This validation-grid sensitivity analysis shows a consistent direction across the
evaluated cells; it does not replace the separately reported held-out confirmation at the focal 2% setting.

4.5    Task and Runtime Trade-offs
Task superiority was not established. Across T = 2/4 accuracy and teacher agreement, deltas were mixed
over the 0.5%-2% residual-budget grid, and the earlier task-superiority target failed. The accepted task
statement is therefore limited to observed degradation rather than universal task gain.
    Using the best matched-budget non-proposed control separately for each dataset and task metric, the
maximum three-seed-mean observed degradations were 0.53, 1.17, and 1.86 percentage points on Cora,
Chameleon, and Squirrel. Across all metrics and seeds, the largest observed single-seed degradation was 2.85
percentage points. These are descriptive maxima under per-metric comparators, not bounds, equivalence
results, or evidence of population-level task preservation.
    A separate secondary benchmark measures warmed full variant construction and evaluation cost at
rank128/1%, not the focal 2% condition. The timed operation includes basis construction, residual con-
struction and selection, reconstruction, rate conversion, and evaluation, while the frozen model, adjacency,
and features are precomputed. Each method-dataset-seed p95 is the 0.95 quantile of seven measured elapsed
times after three warmups, with CUDA synchronization before and after each call. Across nine dataset-seed
pairs, paired p95 full-pipeline runtime ratios versus SVD-only ranged from 1.020 to 1.124, and the maximum
recorded peak CUDA allocation was 218.23 MB. This is a wall-clock pipeline measurement and does not
measure hardware energy.

5    Discussion
Within the reported static-graph setting, the evidence supports a local fidelity-resource result for coding
M0 = ÂX: magnitude-weighted output-Jacobian residual allocation reduced logit MSE under matched
total padded serialized bits, and the maximum observed task degradation remained limited in the evaluated
comparisons. The improvements over the strongest evaluated residual control, our matched-budget A2Q-
style and One-Spike-style adaptations, and the nine positive dataset-seed comparisons against top-absolute
selection are descriptive observations. Squared output-Jacobian sensitivity may preferentially retain residual
directions that influence frozen-model outputs, but this explanation remains a mechanism hypothesis rather
than an identified causal mechanism.
    The evidence is restricted to frozen two-layer hidden-16 GCN checkpoints on Cora, Chameleon, and
Squirrel. The focal setting and logit-fidelity reframing were fixed after validation exposure and before the
test run; the test run is held-out confirmation, not preregistered or independent replication. Sparse residual
coefficients are exact transported-message values rather than temporal SNN events. The study does not
establish formal equivalence, hardware-energy savings, broad GNN generality, downstream utility beyond the
reported metrics, or focal 2% runtime cost. These boundaries constrain both the logit-fidelity interpretation
and the task trade-off.


6    Conclusion
For frozen two-layer hidden-16 GCN checkpoints on Cora, Chameleon, and Squirrel, magnitude-weighted
output-Jacobian coding of the first-layer input message M0 = ÂX improved logit fidelity at matched total
padded serialized bits. At rank 128 and a 2% residual budget, the ratio of three-seed mean split-specific
logit MSE fell by 26.23%, 33.99%, and 25.83% relative to the strongest evaluated matched-budget residual
control. Task superiority was not established; the maximum observed three-seed-mean degradation was
