# P7 structural revision

The Codex ACP draft passed the six-section, evidence-bound, qualifier, and non-truncation checks. Its first validation reported only an internal 2,800-word threshold; the workflow contract has no fixed word target and requires content proportionate to the evidence.

A whole-draft reverse-outline review found one scientific-writing defect: subsection 4.2 repeated the detailed findings later reported in 4.3-4.5. The repeated result paragraphs were merged out of 4.2. That subsection now states only the comparison rule and exact-cost convention. Main results, sensitivity results, negative task-superiority evidence, and runtime/memory findings remain in their distinct result subsections.

No number, unit, aggregation, condition, citation key, uncertainty statement, or scope qualification was changed.
