# C09-v1 rejection record

Status: **reject for the current strong-result portfolio**. The run is preserved as negative evidence; it is not a paper-ready success.

## Evidence

- Command: project Conda Python `-m smoke.run_adaptive_prefix --output smoke/results/C09-v1.json`.
- Unit tests before the driver: `smoke/tests/test_adaptive_prefix.py`, 5/5 pass after the intended RED→GREEN cycle.
- Data: fixed-GCN `dc_mn_val1.npz` and `dc_cf_val1.npz`, complete official validation predictions, no test archive read.
- Frozen partitions: 512 dev, 512 tune, 512 internal validation per dataset; thresholds and fractions selected on tune only.

## Observed outcome

| Dataset | C09 fraction / mean T | T2 acc. | T4 acc. | C09 acc. | C09 teacher agreement | Random p95 acc. | Margin-only acc. |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| MNIST | 1.0 / 4.0 | 91.9922% | 91.6016% | 91.6016% | 99.6094% | 91.6016% | 91.6016% |
| CIFAR10 | 1.0 / 4.0 | 58.9844% | 59.7656% | 59.7656% | 99.2188% | 59.7656% | 59.7656% |

On MNIST, the selected policy upgraded all examples, losing 0.3906 pp accuracy relative to T2 and using mean T=4.0. On CIFAR10 it gained 0.7813 pp over T2 but the same all-upgrade policy was indistinguishable from random/margin-only and violated the mean-T≤3.0 strong gate. Paired bootstrap lower bounds were −1.9531 pp (MNIST) and −1.1719 pp (CIFAR10), so the apparent gains were not robustly positive.

## Decision

C09-v1 fails the frozen minimum/strong gate and is not included among the ten ideas. Its result is evidence that a simple T1/T2 margin + disagreement policy is not a reliable selective-conversion signal on these fixed-GCN outputs. Any future rescue must be a distinct mechanism (for example a calibrated risk bound) and must be labeled C09-v2 with a new preregistered gate; it may not reuse this result as a pass.
