# C10-v1 rejection record

Status: **reject for the ten-idea portfolio**; preserved as a bounded negative result.

## Evidence

- Unit tests before execution: `smoke/tests/test_operator_calibration.py`, 4/4 pass after RED→GREEN.
- Driver: `smoke/run_operator_calibration.py`; output: `smoke/results/C10-v1.json`.
- Checkpoints: 20 frozen local two-layer hidden-16 GCN checkpoints (`chameleon` H1/H2/H3 and `squirrel` H1/H3), four seeds each. The missing Squirrel H2 family was not imputed.
- Training-only scalar factor grid at T=2; validation masks only; test masks never used.

## Aggregate outcome

| Dataset | Baseline mean acc. | C10 mean acc. | Gain | Hosts with positive mean gain | Mean spike ratio |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|---:|
| Chameleon | 47.5309% | 48.0567% | +0.5258 pp | H1 +0.0686, H2 +0.3772, H3 +1.1317 pp | 1.029 |
| Squirrel | 42.7284% | 42.9612% | +0.2329 pp | H1 +0.3906, H3 +0.0751 pp | 1.203 |

The frozen minimum requires +1.0 pp on each dataset; both fail. The strong paired-bootstrap lower-bound requirement also fails: the worst lower bounds are −2.8807 pp (Chameleon) and −2.4038 pp (Squirrel). Several selected factors increase spikes by >10%, especially Squirrel. H3/Chameleon has a positive exploratory signal, but it is not consistent across hosts/seeds and is not admitted as a strong result.

## Decision

C10-v1 is not one of the ten ideas. Do not tune the grid or select a favorable host after seeing these outputs. A rescue would need a genuinely different mechanism and a new preregistration, not scalar operator calibration alone.
