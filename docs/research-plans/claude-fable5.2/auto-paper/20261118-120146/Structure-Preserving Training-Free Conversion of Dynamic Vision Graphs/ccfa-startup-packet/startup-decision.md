# Startup decision

Decision: `pivot-with-rescue-route`.

Rationale: Card A has a concrete discrete-structure mechanism and a clean falsification path, but the imported materials do not establish public licensed teacher weights, a directly runnable DCGS graph implementation, or a baseline-calibrated strong result. Card B depends on unmeasured temporal sparsity and has strong exact-increment competitors. Card C is a viable rescue diagnostic but risks being an incremental audit if no failure appears. Card D has a hardware-model dependence that this local envelope cannot resolve decisively.

Action: enter G0 with Card A narrowed to a directly public dataset and a locally reproducible small dynamic-graph teacher. Treat Card C as the pre-registered rescue route whenever the Card A falsifiers trigger. Do not use unlicensed upstream code in deliverables; reimplement the published operators.

Conditions to convert the startup decision to `proceed`: G0 must identify a current direct baseline path; G1 must verify direct dataset access and license; G2 must define frozen splits and metrics; G3 must identify a falsifying ablation; G4 must separate the proposed work from One-Spike, PMSM, DCGS, and point-cloud conversion; G5-G7 must show local feasibility and a baseline-calibrated strong path; G8 must support a top-tier story without overclaiming.
