# CCFA startup stage plan

- Target tier: top conference; this run will not switch to a lower tier to make a failed idea pass.
- Original method family: training-free ANN-to-SNN conversion for graph neural networks.
- Stage gates: CCFA startup must produce an actionable mechanism card, public-safe opportunity map, red-team verdict, claim-evidence matrix, and strong-result feasibility contract. Only `proceed` enters G0. G0-G4 are hard blocks; G5-G7 are repairable blocks; G8 is the final value gate.
- Artifact owners: pipeline orchestrator owns stage order and decision; idea optimizer owns mechanism cards; literature searcher owns public opportunity map; idea reviewer owns red-team review; experiment designer owns claim-evidence and feasibility contracts; primary agent owns final scientific judgment.
- Decision required at startup: choose one candidate whose mechanism, public data path, strong baselines, local compute envelope, and falsifying ablations can support a top-tier claim. Do not choose a candidate merely because prior documents call it a gap.
- Candidate set: (A) training-free conversion of dynamic kNN/max vision graphs; (B) layerwise thresholded delta inference for streaming visual graphs; (C) failure census across ViG/MobileViG/DGCNN/ST-GCN; (D) fair energy comparison of spiking and quantized GNNs.
- Rejection rule: any candidate without directly public data, a concrete mechanism, a direct baseline path, or a credible strong result returns to startup for replacement; failed candidates remain in the scenario log.
